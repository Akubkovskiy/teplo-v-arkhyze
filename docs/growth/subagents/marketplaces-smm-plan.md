# Marketplaces + SMM plan for 3 houses in Arkhyz

Updated: 2026-04-27  
Owner zone: sales channels, Avito, Yandex, Sutochno.ru, Ostrovok/Bronevik/TravelLine options, SMM  
Project boundary: this is a growth plan for the public Teplo v Arkhyze funnel. Booking operations and live availability remain outside this document and must stay aligned with EasyCamp/owner process.

## Executive summary

Priority for the next 90 days:

1. **Avito as the main paid marketplace**: launch or rebuild 3 separate listings, one per house, with online booking, verified profile/object status where available, fast replies, strict calendar discipline, seasonal pricing, and weekly promotion tests.
2. **Yandex Business / Maps immediately**: create or polish the local business profile because it is free visibility in Yandex Search and Maps, captures route/search intent, and strengthens trust for guests who compare Avito/Sutochno listings against a map card.
3. **Yandex Travel and Sutochno.ru as secondary booking channels**: add after the content package and calendar rules are ready. Yandex Travel supports direct Extranet/channel-manager workflows; Sutochno.ru requires exact pricing, active request handling, and clean calendar synchronization.
4. **Ostrovok / Bronevik through hotel-style readiness**: treat as phase 2, especially if the houses can be represented as categories/units of one accommodation object and legal/classification requirements are settled.
5. **SMM as demand warming, proof, and retargeting material**: VK + Telegram first, short video content for VK Clips and cross-postable reels/shorts where the owner already has access. SMM should feed marketplace conversion, not replace it.

The main risk is operational, not creative: marketplaces reward accurate availability, quick responses, low cancellations, clear pricing, and trustworthy reviews. A beautiful listing with a stale calendar will lose money and may get suppressed or blocked.

- 25-35 horizontal photos per house: exterior, entrance, living room, each sleeping area, bathroom, kitchen, view, evening light, parking/approach, winter/summer variants.
- 8-12 vertical videos: arrival, house tour, morning view, evening lights, nearby routes, weather/snow, guest-ready details.

### Listing copy system

Use one base positioning, then platform variants.

Core promise:

> Three private houses in Arkhyz for mountain weekends: warm interiors, clear rules, real photos, fast replies, and predictable check-in.

Do not overclaim. If a feature is not guaranteed year-round, phrase it as seasonal or availability-dependent.

## Avito playbook

Avito is the main channel because it has broad short-term rental intent and supports transaction-style booking. The operating goal is not just traffic; it is a high-quality profile with reliable listings.

### Listing structure

- Create **one listing per house**, not one mixed listing for all houses.
- Use the same brand naming pattern:
  - "Teplo Arkhyz: private house 1, mountain view, up to X guests"
  - "Teplo Arkhyz: private house 2, terrace, up to X guests"
  - "Teplo Arkhyz: private house 3, family stay, up to X guests"
- First 5 photos: exterior hero, main room, bedroom, view/terrace, bathroom/kitchen proof.
- Title should contain object type + Arkhyz + differentiator. Avoid stuffing.
- Description order:
  1. What this house is best for.
  2. Sleeping/capacity details.
  3. Amenities and heating/Wi-Fi/parking.
  4. What is included in price and what is extra.
  5. Check-in/check-out and house rules.
  6. Nearby points: ski lift, center, shops, routes, travel times only if verified.

### Operations

- Response SLA: first reply within 5 minutes during 08:00-23:00; within 30 minutes otherwise.
- Calendar update SLA: immediately after every direct or off-platform booking.
- Cancellations/refusals: keep minimal; if instant booking is enabled, calendar must be reliable.
- Reviews: ask every satisfied guest for a review within 2-6 hours after checkout.
- Weekly price check: compare visible competitors for the next 3 weekends and major holiday windows.

### Paid promotion tests

Run short tests instead of always-on spend.

| Test | Duration | Budget | Hypothesis | Success metric |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Weekend boost | Thu-Sun, 2 weeks | Small controlled budget | Demand spikes near weekend planning | Cost per booking request and booked nights |
| Holiday window | 21-30 days before holiday | Medium | Guests compare early; higher price tolerance | Net ADR after commission |
| Last-minute fill | 3-5 days before empty dates | Small | Discount + urgency fills weekday gaps | Occupancy of otherwise empty nights |
| Listing photo order | 1 week per variant | No/low paid | Exterior vs interior hero changes CTR | View-to-contact rate |

Do not scale budget until net revenue after commission, cleaning, and owner time is visible.

## Yandex Business / Maps

This should be done even if Yandex Travel is delayed.

### Setup checklist

- Claim or create the organization in Yandex Business.
- Choose category carefully after checking available options: likely "дом отдыха", "глэмпинг", "гостевой дом", "отель", or closest legal/category match.
- Add accurate address/geo point, phone, website, Telegram/bot link if allowed, working hours/contact hours.
- Upload at least 50 photos: exterior by season, interiors, view, road/parking, guest-ready details.
- Add services/attributes: parking, Wi-Fi, family-friendly, pets if allowed, payment methods, check-in info.
- Seed Q&A with real questions: road condition, distance to lifts, pets, max guests, parking, heating, Wi-Fi.
- Reply to every review within 48 hours.

### Why it matters

Guests often validate marketplace listings through maps before paying. A complete Yandex card reduces anxiety and helps with branded/direct searches.

## Yandex Travel

Use Yandex Travel if the object can pass partner onboarding and the owner can maintain exact availability and policies.

### Recommended path

1. Verify legal/classification status first.
2. Register in Yandex Travel Extranet.
3. Decide object model:
   - one accommodation object with 3 house categories/units, if accepted;
   - or separate posutochny objects if that better matches Yandex's current flow.
4. If no channel manager yet, start manually only if there are no more than 2-3 active OTA channels.
5. If adding Ostrovok/Bronevik/Sutochno simultaneously, prefer Bnovo or TravelLine to reduce overbooking risk.

### Setup fields to prepare

- Legal/account info.
- Registry/classification number and link if required.
- House/category names, capacity, bed setup.
- Rate plans: refundable/non-refundable, breakfast/no breakfast if applicable, extra guest policy.
- Restrictions: minimum stay, check-in window, pets/children/parties.
- Payment/cancellation policy.

## Sutochno.ru

Sutochno.ru is useful as a second rental marketplace, but it is strict about operational quality.

### Launch model

- If each house has independent availability and pricing, create each as a separate object.
- If the platform object model is hotel/category-based and accepted, use one parent object with three categories.
- Keep prices exact: base rate, additional guest fee, deposit, cleaning fee, seasonal rates.
- Use calendar sync or channel manager only after object publication and after matching capacity/object types exactly.

### Operating rules

- Process every booking request.
- Keep occupied/unavailable dates closed.
- Avoid price mismatch between listing and actual checkout.
- Watch refusal/cancellation indicators weekly.

## Ostrovok, Bronevik, and channel-manager options

These are phase 2 unless legal/classification and operational readiness are already done.

### When to connect

Connect after Avito + Yandex Business are stable and either:

- direct calendar discipline is proven for 30 days; or
- a channel manager is selected and configured.

### Options

| Option | Best for | Pros | Risks / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manual OTA cabinets | 1-2 extra channels | No software subscription; quick validation | High overbooking risk, duplicated work |
| Bnovo | Small accommodation businesses needing PMS + CM | Supports many Russian channels; channel table includes Avito, Sutochno, Yandex Travel, Ostrovok, Bronevik | Cost and setup overhead; verify actual Avito flow before relying on it |
| TravelLine | Hotel-style sales, official site booking module, strong OTA sync | Fast sync, booking module integration, reporting | May be heavier than needed for 3 houses |
| Bronevik direct | Corporate/B2B and hotel-content distribution | Direct cabinet/contracts and channel-manager API ecosystem | Object type/legal fit must be verified |

Decision rule: do not buy a full PMS/CM only because it is "professional". Buy it when manual updates create measurable risk or owner time cost.

- Posts/videos published.
- Reach.

- Link clicks.

### Decision thresholds

- If a channel brings views but no contacts: change title/photo/price.
- If contacts do not book: fix offer clarity, response speed, price, or rules.
- If bookings happen but net ADR is weak: reduce discounts/promotions or shift availability to direct/other channel.
- If cancellations/refusals rise: stop instant booking until calendar/process is corrected.

## Hypotheses to test

1. **Avito hero photo**: exterior mountain-view hero beats interior hero for CTR.
2. **Instant booking**: instant booking increases booking conversion only if calendar accuracy is near-perfect.
3. **Weekend urgency**: Thursday/Friday promotion fills short gaps better than always-on promotion.
4. **Family positioning**: "private warm house for family weekend" converts better than generic "glamping".
5. **Map trust**: Yandex card with reviews increases marketplace conversion because guests validate the property outside the listing.
6. **Short video**: 20-40 second house tours reduce repetitive questions and increase qualified inquiries.
7. **Direct repeat**: checkout message plus Telegram/VK subscription produces repeat bookings at lower commission cost.

## Risks and controls

| Risk | Impact | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Overbooking across channels | Guest conflict, penalties, bad reviews | One calendar owner, immediate updates, channel manager before scaling |
| Wrong legal/classification status | Listing rejection, advertising risk | Owner verifies registry/classification obligations before hotel-style OTA launch |
| Hidden/incorrect fees | Guest cancellations and platform penalties | Publish all fees: cleaning, deposit, extra guests, pets |
| Slow replies | Lower ranking/conversion | Templates, notification owner, response SLA |
| Too much discounting | High occupancy but weak profit | Track net ADR after commission |
| Marketplace dependency | Commission/platform rule shocks | Build direct site/SMM/review base in parallel |
| Review quality issues | Conversion loss | Pre-arrival clarity, fast issue handling, post-stay review flow |
| Misleading photos | Complaints and refunds | Real current photos, seasonal labels, no overediting |
